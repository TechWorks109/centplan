Getting started

  1. Open Budget Plan. Enter your weekly paycheck and how often it arrives, then tap Set your payday so each month counts its real paydays.
  2. Enter planned amounts for your bills, groceries and other spending.
  3. Use Add Expense and Add Income to log what happens. Overview shows where you stand.

Common questions

How do I log a bill that repeats?

In Add Expense, choose Repeats (daily, weekly, every 2 weeks, monthly or yearly) and a "Repeat until" date. Future entries show as Scheduled and count once their date arrives.

How do I change every repeat of a bill?

Open the entry, choose This and all later repeats, change the amount, and tap Save.

Does my budget sync between my iPhone and iPad?

Yes, if both are signed in to the same Apple ID with iCloud turned on. Check Settings in the app: it shows whether iCloud is syncing. Changes can take a minute to arrive.

How do I start over?

Budget Plan, then the Reset button. Clear Budget Plan sets planned amounts back to zero. Erase Everything & Start Over deletes all data.

How do I back up or move my budget?

Settings, then Save a backup. To restore, choose Restore from a backup. You can also export all transactions as a CSV or a monthly PDF report.

I didn't get a budget alert.

Check that Budget alerts is on in CentPlan's Settings and that notifications are allowed for CentPlan in your device's Settings.
