CentPlan Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

CentPlan is a personal budgeting app. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

We do not collect your data. Your budget stays on your own devices and in your own iCloud account. We have no servers that receive it, and we cannot see it.

What the app stores

The app stores the budget you create: your categories, planned amounts, transactions, notes, savings goals and any receipt photos you attach. This information is saved on your device.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, CentPlan uses Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) to keep your budget in sync between your own devices. That data is stored in your private iCloud database, which is protected by your Apple account. It is not accessible to us or to other users. You can stop syncing by turning off iCloud for CentPlan in your device's Settings.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell or share your personal information.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • We do not require an account.

Notifications, camera and photos

If you allow it, CentPlan sends local notifications on your device when a budget section is close to or over its plan. If you choose to attach a receipt, the app asks to use your camera or photo library, and the photo is stored only with that transaction. You can turn any of these permissions off in Settings.

Exports and backups

When you export a CSV, PDF or backup file, it is created on your device and you choose where to send or save it. We do not receive it.

Deleting your data

You can delete your budget at any time from Budget Plan, then Reset, then Erase Everything & Start Over. Deleting the app removes its data from the device. Data in iCloud can be removed in Settings, then your name, then iCloud, then Manage Account Storage.

Children

CentPlan does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
